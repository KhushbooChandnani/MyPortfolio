Context

Why was this user study important?

1
Lack of User Experience Designer in the team
The team lacked a dedicated User Experience Designer, which means that they never approached product design with a strong focus on user-centered principles.
2
No Usability Testing History
The team did not conduct usability tests on our products' displays before launch, potentially leading to usability issues that went unnoticed.
3
ST3 Usability Problems
The launch of ST3 in the market was marred by significant usability issues, as reported by clients who provided feedback.
4
ST4 Design Improvement
While improvements have been made to the User Interface (UI) of ST4, the team hasn't yet identified and addressed any potential usability issues.
5
Client Expectation for ST4
The client has expressed a strong desire for ST4 to set a new standard in user-friendliness, encompassing both User Interface (UI) and User Experience (UX) aspects.

(Prior to Interviews)

Before conducting user interviews to gather insights into ST3 and ST4, my initial step involved identifying the user interface and user experience issues within these products. This preliminary assessment allowed me to become well acquainted with the existing problems and features, enabling me to engage with users more effectively during the interview process.

Conducted 16 user interviews across departments and age groups within Enovation Controls. To ensure integrity and minimize potential bias, interviews were segmented into different study sequences.

Analysis

Prepared Interaction Maps

I've created interaction maps for both ST3 and ST4. Upon analysis, I've observed that the interaction flow in ST3 appears to be more complex, with multiple interactions requiring more than two clicks. In contrast, ST4 exhibits a more streamlined and organized interface, characterized by interactions that typically involve just two clicks.

Research Synthesis

Analysed Qualitative Data

Following the completion of all interviews and gathering user feedback on ST3 and ST4, I meticulously documented the positive aspects, identified pain points, and collected suggestions from participants.

I then organized these suggestions into distinct feature sets, color-coded them for clarity, and conducted a comprehensive analysis of the identified issues.

UX Recommendations

Performed Heuristic Analysis

After analyzing the issues and in order to increase the efficiency and score of upcoming ST4 launch, I performed a heuristic analysis on the gathered information. It was essential to gain an in-depth understanding of the issues that users encountered while interacting with the systems so I can help pinpoint areas in which improvements are needed.

"The top bar indicators O3, I8, Cleanup and Filter cycle doesn't indicate the icons mean.": P3
Heuristic Violated
Visibility of system status: Indicators should have a pressed state tooltip that indicates the names of the icons as in ST3.
Proposed UX Suggestion
Users should be able to identify the indicators' functionality easily without much cognitive load.
"Third jet speed starts in high-speed state hence it is difficult to analyze first and second jet speeds.": P9
Heuristic Violated
Consistency and standards: It is consistent to achieve the same design and functionality throughout a product.
Proposed UX Suggestion
All the jets should have two speed modes: low and high, and show status "Jet 1 Low speed" so users can easily understand.
"I don't understand that 1, 2, 3, 4 are buttons to set the customized states. The icon looks like a download more than a save button.": P13
Heuristic Violated
Recognition rather than recall: Users should be able to understand the functionalities and features without clicking on a specific button.
Proposed UX Suggestion
The numbers should be unfilled/white and when a customization is applied the number should be filled and the save icon should appear right beside it.
