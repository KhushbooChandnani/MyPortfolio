My Story

I think like a chess player, always a few steps ahead.

I create digital experiences that are intuitive, functional, and thoughtful. With 5+ years in UX and product design, I've worked on everything from surgical tools at Johnson & Johnson MedTech to newborn care apps for global healthcare providers.

"My goal is simple: build things that are easy to use and hard to forget."

Like in chess, I think ahead, understanding user needs, anticipating challenges, and designing solutions that just make sense. I work closely with engineers and product teams, ensuring that what we build isn't just usable, but truly helpful.

I'm looking for a place where my creativity and strategic thinking can thrive. Let's chat about how I can bring design, technology, and empathy together to elevate your product.
